Got Questions? We Have Answers

Everything parents need to know about our Zoom classes, system, trials, and payment.

We offer 1 to 2 free trial classes on Zoom so you and your child can experience Ms. Mai’s interactive coaching methodology firsthand. No payment or credit card is required upfront. If you are satisfied, you can confirm registration.
Every live session is recorded. If a student misses a scheduled class for an excused reason, they receive access to the full recording so they never fall behind. (Note: Recordings are exclusive to enrolled students and not sold separately).
Structure is key: homework is assigned weekly and corrected with individual feedback. We also conduct monthly assessment exams to test comprehension and keep parents informed of their child's academic growth.
Payment is simple and flexible: accepted via InstaPay or Vodafone Cash every 4 sessions in advance. There are no annual lock-ins or installments.
Cohorts are capped at 20 to 25 students per stage. This allows Ms. Mai to engage each student directly, answer individual questions, and apply coaching principles during every lesson.
